# Panora Product

## Audience

Panora is for students, journalists, and everyday readers who want to understand
reports and news through different perspectives without specialist research
skills.

## Purpose

Panora is a personalized, report-grounded AI chat. People begin with the
interaction model they already understand—an AI conversation—and can add reports,
documents, or images as durable context. Panora helps them summarize findings,
compare viewpoints, explain evidence, and identify limitations.

Success means a person can move from a question to a clear, source-aware
understanding without first learning a document-workspace interface.

## Current Product Boundary

- Mobile-only product experience for iOS and Android.
- Reports are the only visible knowledge-source category in phase one.
- News and Research Papers remain future capabilities and have no placeholders.
- Report discovery is available only through the composer’s report picker.
- The report catalog is typed sample data until a backend contract exists.
- File selection is local context, not successful ingestion.
- Voice transcription uses a typed mock adapter until a backend exists.
- Conversations, drafts, feedback, pinned state, context, and theme persist locally.
- The chat endpoint supports cancellable streaming responses.

## Product Principles

1. Familiar before novel: conversation is the primary navigation model.
2. Context stays visible: attached reports and files remain beside the composer.
3. Compact, not cramped: prioritize reading and useful information on a phone.
4. Honest system state: never imply upload, ingestion, or transcription succeeded
   when only local selection or a mock boundary exists.
5. Sources over spectacle: the interface should recede while prose remains easy
   to scan, select, copy, and share.
6. One continuous experience: new chat and active chat use the same composer.

## Brand Commitments

- Panora remains the product name.
- Bricolage Grotesque carries nearly all reading and interface text.
- Anybody is reserved for the Panora wordmark and rare brand moments.
- The product is light-only.
- Warm editorial palettes replace the discarded cool-blue report workspace.

## Accessibility

Panora supports screen-reader labels, scalable text, reduced motion, safe areas,
keyboard and system-back behavior, native permission flows, and minimum effective
touch targets of 44pt on iOS and 48dp on Android.
